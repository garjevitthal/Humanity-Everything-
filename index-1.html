Emergency guidance

If someone is unconscious

  1. Check whether the place is safe before approaching.
  2. Try to get a response and check whether they are breathing normally.
  3. Call 112 and follow the emergency operator's instructions.
  4. If they are breathing normally, place them in the recovery position if you can do so safely.
  5. If they are not breathing normally, begin CPR only if trained or follow the operator's instructions.
  6. Do not give them food, drink, or medicine.
Important: Unconsciousness is a medical emergency. Get professional help immediately. This page is not a substitute for medical care.
